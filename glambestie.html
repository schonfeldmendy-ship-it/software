Questions & answers

What is Glam Bestie?

A free AI makeup coach that works in the browser on any phone — nothing to install. A 30-second face scan (front and both sides) finds your undertone (cool, neutral, warm or olive), face shape and skin depth, gives you a personal Glow Name, and suggests the shades that suit you. Then a voice coach guides makeup looks step by step, showing on your own face where each product goes.

Is it free?

Yes. The face scan, your undertone, face shape and Glow Name are free with no account. With a free account you also get your foundation, concealer, lip and blush shade ranges and a guided look. An optional Premium adds the shades for all 8 products, every look and more daily checks.

Does it work for olive, deep or very fair skin?

Yes. The scan reads olive as its own undertone (the classic warm/cool/neutral quiz leaves it out), and the shade ranges cover very fair to very deep skin. For the most accurate read, face a window in daylight.

Are my photos stored?

No. Face photos are used only to read your coloring and are never stored, and there are no ads in the app. The full privacy policy is at glambestie.com/privacy.

Which languages does it speak?

Glam Bestie works in 15 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Hebrew and Arabic.

Who is it for?

Women 18 and over who want makeup that suits their own coloring — from beginners learning a five-minute look to people choosing the right foundation, blush or red lipstick.
