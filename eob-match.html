Questions & answers

What is an EOB and why compare it to my bill?

An EOB (Explanation of Benefits) is the statement your insurer sends showing what was billed, what they paid, and what you owe. Medical bills often do not match the EOB — comparing them is how billing errors are caught.

What does the dispute kit include?

A summary of the mismatches it found between the bill and the EOB, organized so you can question the charges with the provider or insurer.

Is my medical information uploaded?

No. Bills and EOBs are read locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Is it free?

The first case is free. Unlimited cases are a $29 one-time purchase — no subscription.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. It is a comparison tool; decisions about payment and disputes remain yours.
