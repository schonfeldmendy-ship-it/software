Questions & answers

What does FileCheck look for in a filing?

Personal identifiers that court rules require redacted before filing — Social Security numbers, dates of birth, names of minors, financial account numbers — the categories covered by Federal Rule 5.2 and its state equivalents.

Are my legal documents uploaded to a server?

No. The scan happens locally in your browser. The document never leaves your computer, which is the point: client filings should not be pasted into websites.

Is FileCheck legal advice?

No. It is a checking tool that flags likely identifiers for a human to review. The filer remains responsible for the filing.

How much does it cost?

Basic file-hygiene checks are free. The full Rule 5.2 audit is a $79 one-time purchase per user — no subscription.
