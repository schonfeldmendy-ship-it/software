Questions & answers

What is SignUpSheet.page?

A free website for online sign-up sheets and group date polls. An organizer makes a sheet in about 30 seconds and shares one link; people sign up from any phone. There are 16 ready templates — from potlucks and volunteer shifts to parent-teacher conferences.

Is it really free?

Yes. Sign-up sheets, date polls, slot limits, automatic waitlists, reminder emails, calendar buttons, templates and CSV export are all free, with no ads. An optional paid plan may be offered later, but the core tool is free.

Do people need to create an account?

No — nobody needs an account. People sign up straight from the shared link, and the organizer manages the sheet through a private admin link sent by email.

What happens when a slot is full?

Slot limits prevent double-booking, and an automatic waitlist moves the next person up if someone cancels. Each participant also gets a private link to edit or cancel their own sign-up.

Does it send reminders?

Yes — a reminder email goes out the day before, and every sheet has Google, Apple and Outlook add-to-calendar buttons.

Is my data private?

There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and analytics are cookieless. Sign-up details are stored only to run your sheet and are never sold. The full privacy policy is at signupsheet.page/privacy.
