Questions & answers

How do I save all my open tabs as one PDF?

Install Save All Tabs as PDF from the Chrome Web Store, open the tabs you want, and click the extension button. It prints every open tab and can merge them into a single PDF with a bookmark for each tab.

Is the PDF real text or just a picture of the page?

Real text. Pages are printed the way the browser prints, so the PDF stays searchable and selectable.

Are my tabs or page contents uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser on your computer. No page content, address or title leaves the machine.

Is it free?

The free version handles up to 5 tabs per job. The Pro version is a $12 one-time purchase — no subscription — and removes the limit.
