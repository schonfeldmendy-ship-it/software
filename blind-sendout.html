Questions & answers

What is a blind resume?

A resume with identifying details — name, contact details and similar — removed or masked, so a candidate can be evaluated on experience first. Recruiters send blind versions to clients to keep the process fair and protect the candidate.

Are candidate resumes uploaded anywhere?

No. The resume is processed locally in your browser. Candidate data never leaves your computer.

Is it free?

The free version handles 3 resumes. Unlimited is a $49 one-time purchase — no subscription.

Where do I install it?

From the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store, for Microsoft Edge.
